# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Smart Bookmark serves Chrome and Edge users who rely on many browser bookmarks and want to search, organize, clean, and back them up without sending their browsing data to a hosted service. They use the extension from a new-tab dashboard and a compact side panel, often through keyboard shortcuts.

## Product Purpose

Provide a focused, local-first bookmark workspace: fast bookmark and history search, practical bookmark management, cautious cleanup, and portable backups. Success means the core workflows are trustworthy and easy to reach while permissions, personal data, and unrelated utilities stay out of the product.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, precise. The interface should feel like a dependable browser-native productivity tool: dense when useful, quiet by default, and explicit about risky actions.

## Anti-references

- Author-specific portals, private endpoints, or account parameters shipped as defaults.
- Broad browser permissions justified by low-value peripheral features.
- Unrelated AI, comparison, news, or floating-widget features that distract from bookmark management.
- Decorative dashboard clutter, misleading settings with no active consumer, and irreversible actions without clear confirmation or recovery.

## Design Principles

- Keep bookmark management, search, cleanup, and backup as the visible product spine.
- Prefer local configuration and browser-native APIs over bundled personal defaults.
- Ask only for permissions that an active core workflow demonstrably needs.
- Make destructive operations deliberate, recoverable, and transparent.
- Preserve familiar interaction patterns while keeping the interface responsive and bilingual.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operability. Respect system light/dark preference and reduced motion, retain visible focus states, provide meaningful labels for icon controls, and keep layouts usable in narrow windows. Chinese and English UI should remain functionally equivalent.
