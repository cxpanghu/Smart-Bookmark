Smart Bookmark 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-07-14

中文

书签、设置、历史联想和清理快照在本机处理。开发者不运营接收这些数据的服务器。扩展不申请 Cookie 权限或全域主机权限。

配置天气地点后，扩展会直接请求 Open-Meteo；请求不包含书签或浏览历史。普通搜索会打开用户选择的搜索服务。

English

Bookmarks, settings, history suggestions, and cleanup snapshots are processed locally. The developer does not operate a server that receives this data. The extension does not request Cookie access or broad host access.

After a weather location is configured, the extension calls Open-Meteo directly without including bookmarks or browsing history. A normal search opens the service selected by the user.
